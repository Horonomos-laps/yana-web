New in 1.1

Yana in your menu bar

Click the word Yana for CPU, GPU, memory, free disk space, temperature and what both apps use. Closing the window keeps Yana running; ⌘Q quits.

A wallpaper for Studio

50 animated scenes behind the interface, or your own picture or video.

The two apps connect

Open Studio on the folder you are in. An API key saved once works in both and stays in the Keychain. Chat histories stay separate.

Notch

Music, Claude Code and Codex working or waiting for you, a file shelf, clipboard and timers.

Auto Mode

Works with yana-rt 1.5.6 or later for a short list of commands. A build or test runs project code with your full rights.

13 new providers

More cloud and local models. Projects are kept apart from ordinary chats.

Updates inside the app

From 1.1.0, press Update: the app downloads, checks the signature and reopens.

You choose what it does

The menu bar item, the notch, background running and open at login are switches in Settings. Turn off whatever you do not want.

Install

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Yana into Applications.
  2. On 1.0.0? Press Cap nhat Yana.app in that window.
  3. If macOS blocks the first launch: press Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  4. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple. You do not need to turn off Gatekeeper.
